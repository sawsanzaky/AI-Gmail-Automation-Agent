# 🎯 Executive Overview

The **AI Gmail Automation Agent** is an end-to-end AI automation solution designed to transform unstructured Gmail messages into structured business information.

The platform automatically:

📧 Retrieves emails  
↓  
🤖 Analyzes email content using Generative AI  
↓  
🧠 Classifies and prioritizes messages  
↓  
📝 Generates summaries and suggested actions  
↓  
📊 Structures information into Excel  
↓  
☁️ Synchronizes the Excel workflow with Google Drive  
↓  
📈 Presents results through an interactive Streamlit dashboard

---

# 💼 Business Problem

Organizations receive hundreds or thousands of emails every day.

Manual processing requires employees to:

- Read every email
- Identify the purpose
- Determine priority
- Extract important information
- Summarize the request
- Enter information into Excel
- Track follow-up actions

This creates repetitive work and increases the risk of missed or incorrectly classified information.

---
